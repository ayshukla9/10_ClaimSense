# Checking a medical bill for errors
scope: general

## Common billing errors
Common mistakes include the same service charged twice, a charge for a service or supply you did not receive, the wrong procedure code, and amounts that do not add up. On every line, the amount insurance covers plus the amount you owe should equal the charge.

## How to check a bill
Ask the provider for an itemized bill that lists every service with its date and procedure code. Compare each line with your explanation of benefits from the insurer. Wait for the EOB before paying a large bill, because the amount you owe can change after the insurer processes the claim.

## When a charge looks too high
If you pay half or more of a charge, check whether the provider was in network, whether your deductible was already met, and whether the service needed prior approval. You can ask the provider for a discount or a payment plan, and you can appeal coverage decisions to your insurer.
