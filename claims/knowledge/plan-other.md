# How high-deductible and other plans work
scope: OTHER

## High-deductible plan basics
A high-deductible health plan has a larger deductible than most plans, so you pay the full allowed amount for most care until the deductible is met. In exchange, premiums are usually lower, and the plan may be paired with a health savings account (HSA) for tax-free medical spending.

## What is covered before the deductible
Preventive care from in-network providers is covered at no cost even before you meet the deductible. Most other care, including office visits for a problem, lab tests, imaging and therapy, is paid by you until the deductible is met, then your coinsurance applies.

## Out-of-network care on a high-deductible plan
On the ClaimSense demo high-deductible plan, out-of-network care is covered at a lower rate (the plan pays about 40%) and does not count toward the in-network out-of-pocket maximum, except for emergency care protected by the No Surprises Act.
