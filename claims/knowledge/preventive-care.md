# Preventive care
scope: general

## Preventive care at no cost
Plans that follow the Affordable Care Act must cover a list of preventive services with no copay, coinsurance or deductible when you use an in-network provider. This applies even if you have not met your deductible, including on high-deductible plans.

## What counts as preventive
Examples include a yearly wellness visit or physical, routine vaccines such as the flu shot, blood pressure and cholesterol screening, and recommended cancer screenings. The visit must be for prevention, not for a problem you already have.

## When preventive visits still cost money
If the doctor treats or tests for a new problem during a preventive visit, that part is diagnostic care and your normal deductible and coinsurance apply. Preventive services from an out-of-network provider may not be free. Lab work ordered to investigate symptoms is not preventive.
