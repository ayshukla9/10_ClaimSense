# Insurance terms on your bill
scope: general

## Deductible
The deductible is the amount you pay each plan year for covered care before your plan starts sharing the cost. If your deductible is $1,500, you pay the first $1,500 of covered in-network care yourself. Preventive care on the plan's preventive list is usually covered before you meet the deductible.

## Coinsurance
Coinsurance is your percentage share of a covered charge after you have met your deductible. With 20% coinsurance on a $1,000 in-network charge, the plan pays $800 and you pay $200. Coinsurance is different from a copay, which is a fixed dollar amount such as $25 for a visit.

## Out-of-pocket maximum
The out-of-pocket maximum is the most you pay for covered in-network care in a plan year. Deductible, coinsurance and copays all count toward it. Once you reach it, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the year. Monthly premiums and charges for services the plan does not cover do not count toward it.

## In-network and out-of-network providers
An in-network provider has a contract with your insurer and has agreed to accept the insurer's allowed amount. An out-of-network provider has no contract, so the plan pays less or nothing, and the provider may bill you for the difference. Always check a provider's network status for your specific plan before non-emergency care.

## Allowed amount and balance billing
The allowed amount is the maximum the insurer will consider for a service. Balance billing is when an out-of-network provider bills you for the difference between its full charge and the allowed amount. In-network providers are not allowed to balance bill. Federal rules ban balance billing for most emergency care (see Emergency care and surprise bills).

## Explanation of benefits (EOB)
An explanation of benefits is the statement your insurer sends after processing a claim. It lists the amount charged, the allowed amount, what the plan paid and what you may owe. It is not a bill. Compare the EOB with the provider's bill before paying; the amounts you owe should match.
