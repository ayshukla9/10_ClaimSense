# How HMO plans work
scope: HMO

## HMO network rules
An HMO (health maintenance organization) plan covers care only from providers in its network. You choose a primary care provider (PCP) who coordinates your care.

## Out-of-network care on an HMO
An HMO does not pay for out-of-network care except emergencies, and urgent care when you are outside the plan's service area. If you see an out-of-network provider for non-emergency care, you pay the full bill yourself.

## Specialists on an HMO
You need a referral from your primary care provider before seeing a specialist, and the specialist must be in the HMO network. Without a referral the plan may deny the claim even if the specialist is in network.
