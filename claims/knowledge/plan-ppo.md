# How PPO plans work
scope: PPO

## PPO network rules
A PPO (preferred provider organization) plan lets you see any doctor or specialist without a referral. You pay the least when you use in-network providers, but the plan also covers care from out-of-network providers.

## Out-of-network care on a PPO
On the ClaimSense demo PPO plans, the plan pays about 40% of an out-of-network charge, so you pay about 60% instead of your normal in-network coinsurance. Out-of-network providers can also balance bill you, except for emergency care and the other cases covered by the No Surprises Act.

## Specialists on a PPO
You do not need a referral from a primary care doctor to see a specialist on a PPO plan. Choosing an in-network specialist keeps your cost at your normal coinsurance after the deductible.
